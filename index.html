Bärli's German Grammar & Pronunciation Rules

1. Capitalization Rules (Nouns & Sentences) • ALL single nouns (e.g. das Buch, der Tag) and proper nouns MUST be capitalized! Non-capitalized words are verbs or adjectives (unless starting a sentence).
• Sentence starters and formal Sie are always capitalized.
2. Digraph Rule ("ei" vs "ie") Pronounce the 2nd letter!
• ei = pronounced "eye" (like English i)
• ie = pronounced "ee" (like English e)
3. Sound Swaps: W vs V • German W = English "V" sound (e.g. Wasser → "Vasser")
• German V = English "F" sound (e.g. Volk → "Folk")
4. Missing English "Th" & Vocalized "R" • English "Th" sound does NOT exist in German → replaced with "Z" or "D" ("zis" instead of "this")
• "R" sounds are back-of-throat or vocalized to "ah" at syllable ends (besser → "bessa").
5. Formality: Frau & Herr • Use Frau (Ms./Mrs.) and Herr (Mr.) with formal Sie for adults/teachers. Use informal du for friends/peers.
